Merchant Onboarding

Bitcoin on Main Street

Accept payments with lower fees, get paid instantly, and reach new customers, with zero contracts and zero hidden costs. Jersey City Bitcoin helps local businesses start accepting Bitcoin, hands-on and free.

Why accept Bitcoin

Low fees, no minimums

Receive payments directly from your customers, much like cash. No middlemen taking hefty processing fees.

Instant settlement

Payments settle in seconds. No waiting for your card processor or bank to pay you out.

No chargebacks

Payments happen directly between you and your customer. Chargeback fraud is impossible by design.

New customers

Get listed free on Bitcoin merchant maps and reach people who want to spend bitcoin locally.

It is already happening in Jersey City. At IQ Coffee, 3-4 customers pay in bitcoin every single day. Nationally, over 800,000 Square merchants across America now accept Bitcoin payments.

Three ways to start

Square Want dollars, easiest

Already on Square? Bitcoin over Lightning is built in: 0% processing fee through Dec 31 2026, then 1%. Choose automatic USD conversion and your bookkeeping does not change. New Jersey is supported.

BTCPay Server Want bitcoin, sovereign

Free, open-source, non-custodial: no processing fees, no KYC, funds land straight in your own wallet. A JC Bitcoin volunteer handles the setup and the Lightning plumbing.

OpenNode Online stores

Bitcoin + Lightning checkout for Shopify, WooCommerce, and web stores, with automatic USD payout to a US bank. OpenNode is a JC Bitcoin sponsor.
